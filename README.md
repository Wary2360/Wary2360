## About

### Hyoungwon Seo · 서형원

I am an **M.S. student in Computer Engineering** at Hanbat National University, working in the **Artificial Intelligence & Media Laboratory (AIM Lab)** under the supervision of **Prof. Haneol Jang**.

My research focuses on multimodal computer vision for challenging sensing environments, including infrared and visible imagery, SAR and EO imagery, document intelligence, and media forensics.

📍 Daejeon, Republic of Korea · 🎓 Hanbat National University · 🔬 AIM Lab
